Beta Privacy Notice

Xeero AI Security Guard 0.1.0 Private Beta · Effective October 7, 2026

Scope: This notice explains how Xeero AI handles personal information submitted when you apply for the private beta. The desktop application itself is designed to keep scan history locally on the Windows device.

1. Who is responsible for your application data?

Xeero AI is responsible for the private-beta application information described in this notice.

For privacy requests, please reply to the beta invitation or other beta correspondence sent to the email address you provided in your application.

2. What information do we collect?

When you apply for the private beta, the application form may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Windows version
  • Your reason for wanting to test Xeero
  • User type (for example, individual or business)
  • The date and time your application was submitted

Please do not submit passwords, security tokens, financial information, health information, or other unnecessary sensitive information through the beta form.

3. Why do we use this information?

We use beta application information to:

  • review and select private-beta testers;
  • contact applicants about their application and beta participation;
  • understand the Windows environments represented by applicants;
  • administer and improve the private beta; and
  • respond to beta-related questions and feedback.

4. Legal basis

For beta recruitment and administration, Xeero AI relies on legitimate interests in operating, evaluating, and improving the private beta, balanced against the rights and interests of applicants. Where another legal basis is required by applicable law, we will use the appropriate basis.

5. Where is application information stored?

Private-beta applications are collected using Google Forms and stored in the associated Google Sheets workspace used to administer the beta. Google may process information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's handling of data.

6. How long do we keep it?

We keep beta application information only for as long as reasonably necessary to administer the private beta, handle follow-up, and maintain appropriate beta records. Our target retention period is no longer than 12 months after the private beta closes, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve a documented matter.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable data-protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, and other rights provided by law.

To make a privacy request, reply to the beta correspondence associated with your application and clearly state the request you want to make. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. The Xeero desktop application

Xeero AI Security Guard 0.1.0 is a read-only Windows security-status dashboard. Its scan history is stored locally on the Windows device. The application does not claim to provide comprehensive malware detection, real-time or behavioral malware protection, threat remediation, or automated malware removal.

Windows Update checks may communicate with the computer's configured update source as part of checking Windows Update status. This notice does not change the privacy practices of Windows, Microsoft Defender, Windows Update, Google, or other services used by the operating system.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the beta process or data handling changes. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
